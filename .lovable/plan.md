# Marketing funnel diagram: where owned media sits

A single Mermaid diagram artifact showing the marketing funnel stages and which building blocks operate at each stage, positioning owned media (the Blueprint framework) as the compounding middle.

## What gets created

- `/mnt/documents/Owned_Media_Funnel.mmd` — a Mermaid flowchart, shared back in chat as a rendered diagram artifact.
- No app or code changes.

## Structure

Three funnel bands, top to bottom, with channels grouped under each:

```text
AWARENESS (rented reach)      Paid ads · Social · PR · Events
        |
CONSIDERATION (owned media)   Flagship series · SEO/content library · Community
   the compounding middle     -> Newsletter as the owned audience system of record
        |
CONVERSION & RETENTION        Sales conversations · Lifecycle email · Repeat/referral
```

Plus a feedback arrow from owned media back up to awareness, showing compounding: each owned asset keeps working after spend stops, while paid reach resets to zero.

## Framing rules

- Rented channels (paid, social, PR, events) are labeled as reach you stop having when the budget stops.
- Owned media is the middle layer everything else feeds into and draws from.
- Newsletter is shown as the capture/ownership mechanism, not a stage of its own.
- Node grouping uses subgraphs per funnel stage; a distinct style class marks the owned band so it reads as the center of gravity.
- Colors kept minimal and legible in both light and dark themes.
